# Tia Health

> Tia Health is a Canadian telehealth platform that connects patients with licensed Canadian providers — family doctors, nurse practitioners, specialists, and naturopaths — for on-demand virtual care by phone, video, and secure messaging. It is part of the WELL Health Technologies Corp. clinic network. Tia Health is a consumer-facing virtual-care product; as of this profile it publishes no public developer portal, REST/OpenAPI, GraphQL, MCP server, or HL7 FHIR CapabilityStatement. Interoperability in the Canadian market is stewarded federally through Canada Health Infoway's pan-Canadian FHIR (CA Core / CA Baseline) specifications, which this platform does not publicly surface.

## Services
- Virtual walk-in and family-practice appointments (phone, video, secure messaging)
- Prescription renewals, sick notes, and lab requisitions
- Mental health therapy via partner Focus Mental Wellness
- Provincial coverage: Ontario (OHIP), British Columbia (MSP), Alberta (AHCIP); paid options elsewhere
